Example output of the /available command (one message, entries separated by "; "):

Mohammad Ibrahim, 19237; Chris Lee, 60007; Juan Miguel, will be available 2pm; Sam Carter, will be available tomorrow morning

Format rules:
  available now + ZIP known  ->  Driver Name, ZIPCODE
  available later            ->  Driver Name, will be available TIME
Drivers who are loaded, not available, or have no ZIP yet are not listed.
